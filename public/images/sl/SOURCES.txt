../Sri lanka. This list exists so a slot can be traced back, re-exported at a different size, or
credited. Slot names describe the place they fill, not the photograph, so replacing one is a
one-file job - and everything under seed-* can also be swapped from the admin console instead.

journeys. The rest of the journey covers are photographs already in the site's own set (the four hero
slides, the four seed-package pictures, the Sigiriya and tea journal covers, and about-story).

The four hero photographs double as the covers of the four theme posts in the journal (turquoise
coast, wildlife, culture, golden hour). Each was uploaded to the media library from the file above, so
every cover is byte-identical to the slide it belongs to - change the hero slide and its journal post
together if you swap one.
